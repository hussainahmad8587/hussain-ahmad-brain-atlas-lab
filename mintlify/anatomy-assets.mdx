# Anatomy meshes and attribution

## Source and license

Brain Atlas Lab uses meshes from the [BodyParts3D 4.0 archive](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/README_e.html), PART-OF OBJ set at 99% polygon reduction. The Database Center for Life Science licenses BodyParts3D under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Required attribution:

> BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.

The app adapts the downloaded geometry for grouping, centering, display materials, and interaction. Its educational descriptions are original summaries.

## Coordinate space and coverage

The app uses 57 brain components, two hippocampus meshes, and one pituitary mesh from the source set. The asset record documents the measured coordinate-space check and source identifiers. Brainstem, cerebellum, hypothalamus, hemisphere, lobe, prefrontal, and ventricular selections group verified components from the brain compound; overlapping groupings reuse loaded meshes.

Some named regions are therefore educational groupings rather than isolated source meshes. The repository's `docs/` folder holds the detailed source inventory, coordinate-space check, reference comparison, and known gaps.
